VOXELITH Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 29, 2026

1. Controller

The controller for the VOXELITH app is [LEGAL_COMPANY_NAME], [REGISTERED_ADDRESS] (Netherlands Chamber of Commerce/KvK no. [KVK_NUMBER]). Privacy questions: [PRIVACY_EMAIL]. This policy follows the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) and Dutch law.

2. Stored on your device

Your language choices, streak, XP, learned phrases, review schedule and settings are stored only on your phone. You can use the app without an account.

3. Account, leaderboards and clans

If you create an account we store your email address, your password (on the server only, hashed), display name, learning progress, XP and streak. Your display name and XP are shown to other users on leaderboards and in your clan; clan chat messages are visible to clan members. Legal basis: performance of a contract (GDPR Art. 6(1)(b)).

4. Microphone and speech recognition

The microphone is used only during speaking practice. What you say is transcribed by your device's speech recognition service (Apple on iOS, Google on Android), which may process audio on its own servers. VOXELITH does not receive or store recordings. No AI or cloud translation service is used; translations and pronunciation scores are produced on your device.

5. Purchases

Payments are handled by the App Store or Google Play; we never see your card details. RevenueCat processes purchase receipts and a pseudonymous user ID to verify your subscription. Legal basis: performance of a contract (Art. 6(1)(b)) and legal obligations (Art. 6(1)(c)).

6. Ads (free version)

The free version shows Google AdMob ads. AdMob may process data such as your advertising ID, IP address, approximate location and ad interactions. In the EU/EEA and UK a consent form is shown on first launch; personalised ads are used only with your consent (Art. 6(1)(a)). On iOS you are also asked for tracking permission. You can change or withdraw your consent at any time in You → Privacy choices. No ads are shown to Plus users.

7. Notifications

Reminders are scheduled on your device; their content is not sent to our servers.

8. Recipients and transfers outside the EU

We share data only with processors that help us provide the service: Supabase (accounts and database), RevenueCat (subscriptions), Google (ads) and Apple/Google (stores, speech recognition). Some of them may process data outside the EEA (e.g. in the US); such transfers are protected by the EU–US Data Privacy Framework or the EU Standard Contractual Clauses.

9. Retention

Account data is kept while your account exists. When you delete your account via You → Delete account, your server account and profile are deleted immediately; deletion requests by email are completed within 30 days. Data on your device is removed when you uninstall the app.

10. Your rights

You have the right to access, rectify and erase your data, to restrict or object to processing and to data portability, and you can withdraw consent at any time. Requests: [PRIVACY_EMAIL]. You can also lodge a complaint with the Autoriteit Persoonsgegevens (autoriteitpersoonsgegevens.nl).

11. Children

VOXELITH is not directed to children under 16.

12. Changes

If we update this policy the date above changes and we announce important changes in the app.
